We created the Doughssant: the shape of a croissant with the soul of a Donutste donut.

Nobody was making them when we did. We named it, claimed it and put it out first. Then in November 2024 it went viral, and the whole world started making their own.

Imitation is the highest form of flattery. But there’s only one original, and it’s still made by hand in our kitchen in Lake Mary.
